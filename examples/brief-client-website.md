## Objective

Design and build a marketing website for Acme Corp, a B2B SaaS company
selling workflow automation to mid-market manufacturers.

## Context

- Acme is a new client, signed on retainer.
- They have a logo but no brand guidelines.
- Their existing site is a WordPress template from 2018.

## Constraints

- Must load in under 1.5s on 3G.
- Mobile-first.
- Accessible (WCAG AA).
- Deployable to their existing Netlify account.
- Copy must be approved by their CMO before launch.

## Deliverables

1. Brand mini-guide (colors, typography, voice).
2. Landing page with hero, features, social proof, CTA.
3. About page.
4. Pricing page.
5. Contact form with confirmation email.
6. SEO foundation: sitemap, meta tags, OG images.

## Success Criteria

- Lighthouse score > 90 on all four metrics.
- CMO signs off on copy and design.
- Deployment completed by 2026-04-26.

## Notes for Client CEO-Agent

- Route design through Design-Lead.
- Route copy through Marketing-Lead (coordinate with client's CMO).
- Route deployment through Dev-Lead.
- If legal review is needed for testimonials, escalate.
