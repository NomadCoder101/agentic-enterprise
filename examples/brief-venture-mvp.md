## Objective

Ship a public beta of "Focustrack", a minimal time-tracking app for solo
freelancers, with a landing page and a Stripe-powered paid tier.

## Context

- This is an internal venture.
- We have 4 weeks.
- Goal: 50 paying users by week 6, or sunset.

## Deliverables

1. Product: web app with login, timer, project tagging, weekly report.
2. Brand: name lockup, logo, color palette, tone.
3. Landing page with waitlist and pricing.
4. Onboarding email sequence (5 emails).
5. Stripe integration for the $9/mo Pro tier.
6. Launch plan for ProductHunt, HackerNews, and 3 niche communities.

## Constraints

- Solo-founder budget: under $200 for MVP.
- Stack: prefer boring tech (server-side rendering, SQLite, no microservices).
- All infrastructure must be replaceable within a weekend.

## Success Criteria

- 100 waitlist signups in week 1.
- 10 paying users by week 4.
- Landing page converts at > 3%.

## Notes for Venture CEO-Agent

- Route product through Dev-Lead.
- Route brand and landing page through Design-Lead.
- Route launch and email sequence through Marketing-Lead.
- Route pricing analysis through Research-Lead.
